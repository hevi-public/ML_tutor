Lab: dynamic programming

Coin change, the unit-6 classic: fewest coins to make an amount, greedy provably insufficient on the given denominations. The twist: the harness includes an amount that naive exponential recursion cannot finish inside the sandbox timeout — the timeout is the overlapping-subproblems detector.

Before you start The DP recipe and the coin-change case study (these denominations are the greedy-breaking set).
Why a timeout is a fair grader

"It works on small inputs" is the archetypal DP bug report: correctness and complexity are separate properties, and only the second one pages you at 3am. amount=800 with 5 coin types has an exponential recursion tree but only 801 distinct subproblems — the gap between those two numbers is the entire lesson of unit 6, and here it's the difference between 4ms and the heat death of the sandbox.
