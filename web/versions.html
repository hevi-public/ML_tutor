Versions & support windows

Which version is current, when yours stops being supported, and what landed in each release. Dates come from the npm registry, so they are the real publish dates rather than the blog-post dates.

At a glance
  • Angular ships twice a year, roughly May and November. Each major gets 6 months of active support, then 12 months of LTS — so a version is done 18 months after release.
  • RxJS 7.8 is the answer. RxJS 8 has been in alpha since 2025 and is deliberately on hold until Observable is standardised on the web platform. Do not plan around a date.
  • Vitest majors land roughly yearly. Angular pins a compatible range through @angular/build, so in an Angular repo you usually upgrade Vitest by upgrading Angular.
  • pnpm 11 moved its configuration. If you upgrade from 10, settings in package.json and .npmrc stop being read — see the pnpm section.

Are you still supported?

Angular's support window is the one that bites, because it is the only one of the four with a published clock. Find your major in the Angular table below: a version marked out of support receives no further patches, including security patches.

The short version Angular 19 left support in May 2026. If you are on 19 or below, upgrading is a security matter, not a features matter. Angular 20 and 21 are in LTS (fixes only). Angular 22 is the active line.

Upgrading

Angular upgrades are one major at a time — ng update will refuse to skip. Each step runs the schematics that rewrite your code for that version's changes, which is why skipping majors by editing package.json by hand costs far more than it saves.

What you wantCommand
See what an upgrade would do, without doing it ng update
Step to the next major ng update @angular/core @angular/cli
Step to a specific major ng update @angular/core@21 @angular/cli@21
Re-run a single migration you skipped ng update @angular/core --migrate-only --from=21 --to=22

The interactive, version-to-version checklist is on the official update guide, and the per-version migration schematics get their own page in this reference.
