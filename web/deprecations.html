What changed

Every recorded change across the four tools: what to use now, what it replaced, when it happened, and whether a command will do the rewrite for you.

How to read this
  • Since is the version the new form became available — not necessarily the version it became the default.
  • Deprecated means it still works and still ships, but is receiving no further work and has a stated replacement.
  • Removed means upgrading past that version breaks your build. These are the rows to plan around.
  • An empty migration column means the change is not a mechanical substitution — a person has to make a judgement call per call site.
