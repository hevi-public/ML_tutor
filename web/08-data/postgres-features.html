Features by version

The SQL you can write depends on the major you run. Each card is one capability with the version it landed in — set the filter to your production major and the badges tell you what's already yours.

At a glance
  • On 15+ you have MERGE.
  • On 17+ you have JSON_TABLE and incremental backups.
  • On 18 you have uuidv7(), virtual generated columns, skip scan and RETURNING old/new — 18 is unusually rich in every-day-SQL features.
  • Upsert guidance: plain two-case upsert stays INSERT … ON CONFLICT; MERGE is for decision tables.

What changed

Gotcha: features vs managed-Postgres lag A feature existing in the major is necessary, not sufficient: managed providers trail new majors by weeks-to-months, and extensions lag further. The version filter on this page tells you what the engine can do — your platform's supported-version page tells you when you can have it.
