What landed in each version, newest first. Useful in two directions: working out what you gain by upgrading, and working out why the code in front of you was written that way.

Reading old code If you have inherited a codebase, the fastest way to date it is to look at which of these it uses. A component with *ngIf and a destroy$ subject is pre-v17 in style regardless of what its package.json claims.
